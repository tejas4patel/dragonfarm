# Support

dragonfarm is free and open source (MIT), looked after by one person, as
time allows. There is no paid support and no service-level promise.

## Getting help

- **Something broke.** Open an [issue](https://github.com/tejas4patel/dragonfarm/issues)
  with the output of `dragon_check()`, what you did, and what happened. For
  a training run, the run's `log.txt` helps most.
- **A security problem.** Not in a public issue: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **A question or an idea.** An issue is fine; the feature form asks what
  you are trying to do, which matters more than how.

## Which versions

Only the latest release on CRAN and the `main` branch on GitHub are looked
after. Fixes go into the next release; older releases are not patched, and
there are no long-term-support releases.

## For a team or a company

You can use, change and ship dragonfarm under the MIT licence, including
inside a validated system. Validation is yours to do: the package gives
you a qualification report, an append-only record, and the
measurements behind its claims, but nobody checks your deployment for
you. If you need guaranteed answers, plan for your own people to own it.
